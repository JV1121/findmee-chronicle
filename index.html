JV spent nearly two decades operating at the intersection of consulting, management, analytics and large-scale business transformation, where operational discipline and complex decision-making are measured in millions of dollars and thousands of moving parts.

He learned to take fragmented information, complex operations and seemingly impossible business problems, and turn them into systems that work.

But his most personal and most ambitious chapter did not begin in a boardroom.

What if the same principles used to connect data, people and operations inside the world's largest companies could be redirected toward a problem where the outcome is not revenue — but a human life?

The question was simple. The answer became FindMee.

Chapter 02 · The Crisis In Numbers

India has a crisis that rarely receives sustained attention.

This is not a statistic. It is a national emergency — one that has been quietly running in the background of the country's growth story for decades.

After a First Information Report is filed, citizen participation remains nil. Millions of people may want to help, but they simply don't know how to help.

For someone who had spent years building enterprise systems, the gap was striking. India does not lack people. It lacks the mechanism to mobilize the attention of those people at the right moment.

That question — what is the mechanism? — became the beginning of FindMee.

Chapter 03 · The Object

India's first
water with a voice.

FindMee, built under HopeViara, is an FMCG and technology-led initiative designed to turn an everyday consumer touchpoint into an opportunity to help find a missing person.

i.
Every bottle carries a QR code. Linked to a real missing-person profile — including AI-enhanced and age-progressed visuals.
ii.
One scan. One sighting. A consumer sees who to look for. If they recognize someone, they report a potential sighting with geo-location.
iii.
100% glass. Zero plastic. Premium mineral water in sustainable packaging. Designed for the shelf, engineered for the search.
iv.
Every consumer is a search agent. Deliberately uncomplicated: put the missing in front of more eyes, and make helping easy.

We're turning every bottle into a nationwide search engine.
No algorithm. No supercomputer. Just 1.4 billion people watching,
and staying united for one purpose.

Bring Them Home.

The Mechanism

From awareness to action.

i

Consumer buys a bottle.

Premium glass, mineral water. ₹1 per bottle routed to search operations.

ii

QR scan reveals a person.

A real missing-person profile, with AI-enhanced and age-progressed visuals.

iii

Sighting reported with geo-tag.

If the consumer recognizes someone, one tap files a location-stamped lead.

iv

AI matches. Family notified.

Reports are analysed against records, face-matched, and routed to authorities.

04

Beyond a product. An intelligence engine.

At its heart, FindMee reflects the same strategy and thinking that defined JV's corporate career. Fragmented data becomes intelligence. Disconnected touchpoints become a network. Information becomes action. And scale is designed into the model from the beginning.

Technology, however, is not the story. People are.

Stage 01
From data — QR scans, citizen reports, image inputs collected at national scale.
Stage 02
To intelligence — geographic hotspots, behavioural patterns, risk maps of India's missing.
Stage 03
To intervention — for governments, law enforcement and NGOs.
Stage 04
To recovery — the only metric that matters.
